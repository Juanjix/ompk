## 03 — Reading it plainly

This is a much cleaner result than Orson's. Nothing found suggests OMPK is already someone else's clothing brand or registered trademark, in Argentina or elsewhere. The two open items are administrative, not red flags:

1. **Confirm formally with INPI** — the authoritative registrability answer has to come from an actual INPI search or a trademark agent, not an absence of Google results. Direct link: https://portaltramites.inpi.gob.ar/marcasconsultas/busqueda/
2. **Pick a domain/handle strategy** — `ompk.com.ar` looks open and is the natural home for an Argentine label; `ompk.com` exists but is parked (a broker will likely want a premium price for it — worth a quick, no-commitment inquiry, not worth overpaying for). For Instagram, plan on a variant handle (e.g. `@ompk.ba`, `@wearompk`, `@ompkstudio`) since the bare `@ompk` is taken by an unrelated account.

## 04 — Recommended next actions (manual — these are the founder's to execute, not something to do on your behalf without confirmation)

1. Run the direct INPI search for "OMPK" in class 25 (clothing) and class 35 (retail/commerce), or have a trademark agent do it — this is the one item that actually needs an authoritative answer before filing anything.
2. Register `ompk.com.ar` via NIC.ar if the direct check confirms it's open.
3. Decide on `ompk.com`: inquire about the parked domain's price, or commit to `.com.ar` / another TLD as primary and skip it.
4. Secure an Instagram (and other social) handle variant now, even before launch — handle squatting is common and costs nothing to prevent early.
