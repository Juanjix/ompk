# PHASE 02C — Naming Round 03 / Attitude Before Explanation

Status: exploration only. `[BRAND]` in `docs/BRAND_BIBLE.md` is still unresolved. Rounds 01 and 02 are preserved as research. This round formalizes a reference set of 20 names supplied directly by the team, in a shorter, more universal register than Rounds 01–02, alongside the visual/product reference that prompted it.

## 01 — What triggered this round

Two references came in together:

1. **GMK's Mick Flare Jeans** (denim, pinstripe, black corduroy, white, striped hickory) — confirms the bootcut/flare trouser as a real, sellable silhouette at the right fit: five-pocket construction, pewter hardware, flare breaking at the boot. This has been folded into `docs/BRAND_BIBLE.md` §20–21 as the hero-garment fit reference (GMK cited there only as a market comparable, not a direction to imitate).
2. **A "Naming Round 03" mockup** — 20 names, much shorter and more international than Rounds 01–02 (human first names, surnames, places), scored on just Fashion / Attitude, presented as a magazine tearsheet rather than a scored dossier. That register — and its two taglines, **"Attitude before explanation"** and **"Clothes for a louder tomorrow"** — are strong on their own, independent of which name wins, and are logged here for reuse regardless of outcome.

This round takes those 20 names at face value and pressure-tests them the way Rounds 01–02 tested theirs — including flagging where a name already means something else in the world, which the original mockup's scoring didn't account for.

## 03 — Refined shortlist (6), deeper read

### Morel
Real, unclaimed-feeling surname — sounds like it's already a small fashion house. Fashion 9, Attitude 9, no meaningful conflict found. On the trouser: excellent, short and confident. Reads slightly more European than Argentine on its own — leans on photography and casting to place it in Buenos Aires.

### Sabino
Warm, could be a first name or a surname, Italian-immigrant resonance without spelling it out. Fashion 8, Attitude 8. Softer and more approachable than Morel — better fit if the brand wants warmth over aloofness.

### Cavan
Short, hard-edged, genuinely obscure enough (an Irish county) to be fully ownable in fashion with zero prior baggage. Fashion 8, Attitude 8. The least "explained" of the six — closest in spirit to Round 02's Territory F logic (meaning built by the brand, not required upfront).

### Salvo
Clean and relaxed, reads naturally Argentine without leaning on slang. Fashion 8, Attitude 8. Doubles quietly as "except/save" in Spanish — a small, optional layer of meaning that doesn't have to be explained to work.

### Malva
Soft color-and-plant name, gentler register than the other five — the closest thing in this round to Round 01/02's Ámbar (a color that already exists in the brand's own palette logic). Fashion 8, Attitude 8. Slight risk of skewing more feminine-coded than the other candidates; worth testing on the actual hero trouser mockup before assuming it's neutral.

### Selva
"Jungle" — sensual and enigmatic, matches the Round 02 instruction that a name doesn't need a ready-made explanation to be desirable. Fashion 8, Attitude 9. Best sensuality-to-cleanliness ratio of the six.

## 04 — Taglines worth keeping regardless of the name

From the reference mockup, independent of which candidate wins:

- **Attitude before explanation.**
- **Clothes for a louder tomorrow.**
- **The right name makes good pants sound better.**

These read as genuine brand copy in the voice Section 27–28 of the Brand Bible already asks for (short, observational, confident, no generic fashion-speak) and are worth holding onto for launch copy regardless of the naming outcome.

## 05 — Master shortlist across all three rounds

Pulling the strongest survivor from each round into one list, for the actual decision conversation:

| Name | Round | Why it's still standing |
|---|---|---|
| **Ámbar** | 02 | Warm, sensual, already inside the brand's own color system; no explanation required. |
| **Ruggeri** | 02 | Sounds like an existing fashion house; a surname's implied history without naming anyone real. |
| **Ultramar** | 01 | The only name that ties Buenos Aires's own port-city identity to something that travels internationally without translation. |
| **Zurdo** | 02 | Four letters, built-in attitude, distinctly Argentine slang without tourist cliché. |
| **Encore** | 02 | Most explicit music reference of the group; highest international legibility of all candidates so far. |
| **Morel** | 03 | Cleanest, most fashion-house-ready surname of the new batch; zero conflict found. |
| **Cavan** | 03 | Fully unclaimed, short, ownable — closest to a blank surface the brand can define entirely on its own terms. |
| **Selva** | 03 | Best sensuality-to-cleanliness ratio in Round 03; fresh territory for fashion specifically. |

Eight candidates now sit at "clean and strong" across three different naming logics — a person's world (Ruggeri, Morel), the city's own identity (Ultramar), attitude/slang (Zurdo), music culture (Encore), sensory color (Ámbar), and pure ownable sound (Cavan, Selva). That range is itself useful: it means the eventual choice is a real decision between distinct positioning strategies, not a coin flip between near-duplicates.
